Greenline Landscaping

WordPress Developer & Designer

Tech Stack

WordPress, Elementor Pro, Custom Post Types, ACF, CSS Grid

Live Site:

https://greenlinelandscaping.com

A visually driven business site for a landscaping company that wanted to showcase the transformation their work creates — built on Elementor with custom post types powering a filterable project gallery and a structured service area directory.

The Problem

Greenline’s previous website was a five-page brochure that treated every service the same and buried their strongest selling point — dramatic before-and-after project photography — in a single static gallery page. Potential clients couldn’t filter by service type, neighborhood, or project scale. The site generated almost no inbound leads despite the company’s strong local reputation.

The Build

I designed and developed the site on WordPress with Elementor Pro, structuring the content architecture around two custom post types: Projects and Service Areas. Each project entry carries ACF fields for location, service category, square footage, and a before-and-after image pair. Service areas are geo-tagged and cross-linked to relevant projects, so a visitor browsing “Patio & Hardscape” sees completed work in their neighborhood without searching.

The gallery uses CSS Grid with Elementor’s loop builder to render a masonry-style layout that adapts fluidly across breakpoints. Image assets are served through responsive srcset attributes, and lazy loading keeps the initial page weight under 1.2MB despite the photography-heavy design.

Results

Within three months of launch, Greenline reported a 40% increase in quote requests through the site. The before-and-after gallery became the most visited section, with an average session duration nearly triple that of the old site. The service area taxonomy now powers their Google Business Profile landing pages, driving local SEO improvements across eight zip codes.

Scope

  • Custom project gallery with before-and-after comparison
  • Service area CPT with geo-taxonomy
  • Elementor loop templates for dynamic archive pages
  • Performance optimization under 1.2MB initial load
  • SEO-tuned landing pages per service area
